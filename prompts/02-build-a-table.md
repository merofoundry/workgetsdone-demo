# Tutorial 02: Build Your First App Table With One Prompt

This prompt creates a new app called WorkGetsDone with one table, Companies, and three sample companies.

First connect your AI assistant to MeroFoundry: copy the connect prompt from your own Start page and paste it into the assistant. It is not shown here because it is specific to your account.

Then paste this prompt:

```text
I want to start a new app for my consulting business. Call it WorkGetsDone, with workgetsdone as the short name I'll see in the URL.

Then give it one table to start: Companies. Each company needs a name, and optionally what industry they're in. Add three to begin with: Meridian Group (software), Halstead and Co (insurance), and Bluewater Labs (biotech).

Do the whole thing in one go. I only want to approve this once, and I'd like to know when it's done.
```
